# Design diagnosis — why the v1 public site feels flat

Status: research deliverable R1 (feature `public-site-redesign`). Evidence: full-page Playwright captures of the local v1 site taken 2026-11-04: the home page at 1440 px desktop, the home page at 390 px mobile (12,682 px tall), and a room detail page at desktop width. They were compared against the references in [`design-references.md`](./design-references.md). The table below records what those captures showed, so this document stands on its own. The image files (`public-wa-home-desktop.png`, `public-wa-home-mobile.png`, `public-room-detail.png`) were transient evidence and are deleted when the feature closes; see [`design-research.md` § Screenshot policy](./design-research.md#screenshot-and-copyright-policy).

## Headline

**v1 is a correct, accessible component library laid out as a SaaS landing page, not a place.** Every section uses the same recipe (H2 left, link right, grid of rounded cards on a flat band). Typography, imagery and motion are all set to "safe", so nothing is memorable. The fix is not more effects. It needs a point of view: a stronger type scale, deliberate framing of the portrait photos, one narrative thread (Mocoa, bamboo, rest), and two or three signature moments.

Notes:

- The floating "N" circle in the captures is the Next.js dev indicator, not part of the design.
- Some emptiness is **content**, not layout. The hero kicker, subtitle, about-story and location blocks render only when `municipality`, `shortDescription`, about text and location data exist, and those values are still pending hotel confirmation in the seeds. The redesign must look complete with *minimal* confirmed content, so the layout needs to carry the page without them (rows 1, 7 and 10).
- The gallery-to-FAQ gap is two stacked `--section-y` paddings, left behind because the location section is skipped. The section spacing should collapse when neighbours are hidden.

## Ten highest-impact changes (ordered by impact ÷ cost)

1. **Rewrite the hero as a place-first statement.** Use a portrait **diptych/triptych** (desktop) or a full-height plate (mobile), a display headline that names Mocoa and the promise (use the confirmed tagline, *"descanso, comodidad y confianza en el corazón de Mocoa"*, once the hotel approves it), a kicker with the location, and **one** brand-coloured CTA plus a quiet secondary. *(Paradero, Odisej)*
2. **Double the display scale and add a label voice.** Fluid display `clamp(3rem, 8vw, 8.5rem)` for one or two statement lines per page. Add tracked uppercase or small-caps kickers and numbered section markers ("01 — Habitaciones"). Fix the font payload at the same time (see art-direction briefs: −60 to −100 KB).
3. **Give portrait photos a framing system instead of cropping them.** Use 3:4 / 4:5 "plates" with captions, staggered pairs, arched or segment masks for one or two hero moments, and never landscape-crop a 960×1280 image to fit a card.
4. **Break the zebra rhythm.** Add at least three changes of pace: a giant-type statement band, a pinned/sticky storytelling section, a full-height image moment, and an asymmetric editorial spread.
5. **Turn services into the "honest differentiators" story.** Solar power, water reserve and internet on every floor go into a dedicated illustrated or numbered section. Use custom line illustrations (bamboo-stroke style) drawn on scroll, not Lucide-in-circles.
6. **Demote WhatsApp neon.** The primary CTA uses the brand colour (bamboo-700/900 or cane on dark) with a WhatsApp glyph. Keep `#25D366` only for the small FAB icon, or drop it. Use a persistent but discreet "Escríbenos" pill in the header.
7. **Introduce a tactile layer.** A subtle grain overlay (static SVG/PNG noise, `mix-blend-mode`, <3 KB), bamboo node/segment dividers, and an organic edge (wave or segment notch) at one or two transitions.
8. **Re-think room presentation.** Use a numbered "índice" list (desktop hover reveals the photo) or tall 3:4 plate cards with name, capacity and price line. Collapse amenity icons into one shared line ("Todas incluyen…") instead of repeating them per card.
9. **Add a sense of place.** Use a typographic "Cómo llegar a Mocoa" moment and a stylised map or route line, real nearby references only when the hotel confirms them, and the sign (#35) as the identity anchor.
10. **Choreograph motion around 3–4 signature moments, not uniform fade-ups.** Hero line reveal, clip-path plate reveals, an SVG bamboo stroke drawing, and one pinned sequence (desktop only). Everything else stays still. Gate it all behind `prefers-reduced-motion` per req. 8.3.

## What v1 got right (keep)

- Token system, contrast table and Spanish copy discipline (identity.md).
- No empty sections when content is missing, consent-gated map, and no-JS-visible content.
- Header and FAB behaviour, a11y focus states, and e2e coverage. The redesign is a **visual and layout layer** on top of this.
