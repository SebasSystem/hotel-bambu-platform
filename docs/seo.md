# Technical SEO and performance

What the public site implements for search engines, how to check it after a deploy, and what only the hotel owner can do off-site. Strategy and sources: [`docs/research/seo-content-strategy.md`](research/seo-content-strategy.md). Implemented in task D6 of `odd/tasks/public-site-redesign.md`.

## 1. Deployment flags

| Variable | Effect |
|---|---|
| `SITE_URL` | Production origin (e.g. `https://hotelbambuplaza.com`). Canonicals, Open Graph URLs, the sitemap and JSON-LD use it. |
| `SITE_INDEXABLE=true` | Allows indexing. Without it every page answers `noindex, nofollow` and `robots.txt` is `Disallow: /` (staging and local copies). |
| `LEGAL_APPROVED=true` | The legal pages lose their draft banner and `noindex`, and join the sitemap. |

All three are read per request, so changing them needs a restart, not a rebuild.

## 2. What is implemented

### Metadata (`apps/web/lib/seo/`)

- **Titles and descriptions** come from the Spanish templates of the research (§7) and real data only (`page-seo.ts`, `room-type-seo.ts`). A fact the hotel has not filled in (rate, beds, schedule, distances) is left out, never invented.
- **Length limits** (`limits.ts`): titles ≤ 60 characters, descriptions ≤ 155. The builders try less detailed variants first (room titles drop the city, then the capacity). Optional sentences that would overflow are skipped, never cut mid-sentence. Unit tests cover every page with long data, and an e2e test checks the rendered pages: one H1, a unique title and description within the limits, and the canonical.
- **Canonical**: absolute and self-referential on `SITE_URL`, without the query string. So `/habitaciones?tipo=pareja` and `/galeria?categoria=…` point to their base page.
- **Language**: `<html lang="es-CO">`, `og:locale` `es_CO`, `WebSite.inLanguage` `es-CO`.
- **Open Graph and Twitter**: `summary_large_image`, using a real hotel photo (hero slides, else gallery, else room covers; the largest WebP variant with its alt text). Room pages use the room cover. The fallback is `public/brand/og-default.png` (1200×630, new brand), which is generated by code: `pnpm --filter @hotel-bambu/e2e og-image` while the web is running (set `CHROME_PATH` on a host without Playwright's Chromium).
- **Icons**: `app/icon.svg` (favicon), `app/apple-icon.tsx` (180 px PNG) and `app/manifest.ts` (`/manifest.webmanifest`: name, colours, icons).
- **noindex**: admin (layout metadata plus `X-Robots-Tag` from `proxy.ts`), legal drafts (until `LEGAL_APPROVED`), 404 (`noindex, follow`), and the whole site without `SITE_INDEXABLE`.

### Crawling

- **`robots.txt`** (`lib/seo/robots.ts`): production allows `/`, disallows `/admin` and `/api/`, and lists the sitemap. `/_next/` stays crawlable so Google can render the pages.
- **`sitemap.xml`** (`lib/seo/sitemap.ts`) lists the home page, rooms index, every published room type, location, gallery and policies, plus the legal pages once approved. `lastModified` comes from data only: the room type's last edit, the hotel profile (location), or the newer of the two (home, rooms index). Pages without a data date carry no date. Redirected URLs are never listed.
- **Redirects** (`lib/public/redirects.ts`, `next.config.ts`): `301` from `/contacto` to `/ubicacion`, from `/preguntas-frecuentes` to `/politicas-del-hotel`, and from `/servicios` to `/`. These are every public page removed since v1, checked against the git history. Old per-room and renamed room-type slugs answer a `308` permanent redirect to the type page through the API slug map. Google treats a 308 like a 301. All redirects land on a live page in one hop.
- **404**: a custom page inside the site shell with status 404 and `noindex, follow`. It links to the rooms, the contact CTA, the main sections and every room type.

### Structured data (`lib/seo/json-ld.ts`)

| Page | JSON-LD |
|---|---|
| Home | `WebSite` + `Hotel` (`@id` `/#hotel`) |
| Location | `Hotel` (same `@id`) + `BreadcrumbList` |
| Room type | `@graph`: `HotelRoom` (`containedInPlace` the hotel) + its `Offer` when priced + `BreadcrumbList` |
| Rooms, gallery, policies, legal | `BreadcrumbList` |

- **Hotel**: `name` and `PostalAddress` (Google's required fields for LocalBusiness), plus `geo`, `telephone`, `url`, `image`, `amenityFeature`, `numberOfRooms` and `checkinTime`/`checkoutTime` when set. It also has `containsPlace` (one `HotelRoom` per published type), `makesOffer` and `priceRange` (only when rates exist), and `sameAs`. There is no `aggregateRating` or `review` (self-serving reviews are ineligible), no `FAQPage` (no rich result since May 2026) and no `openingHours` (Google Business Profile says hotels should not set business hours).
- **Rooms are `HotelRoom`, not `["HotelRoom","Product"]`.** A `Product` with an `Offer` makes Google evaluate the page for merchant listings and product snippets, which then warn about shipping, returns and availability that a hotel does not have. The rate is linked the way the schema.org hotels guide allows: Hotel `makesOffer` Offer, Offer `itemOffered` HotelRoom. The Offer has `businessFunction` LeaseOut and a `UnitPriceSpecification` in COP per `DAY`. `occupancy` uses unit code `IE` (persons).
- **NAP**: the footer, the location page and the JSON-LD read name, address and phone from one source (`lib/public/nap.ts`). Unit and e2e tests compare them.

### Content semantics

- One H1 per page that matches the title's intent, and a logical heading order (axe and Lighthouse accessibility show no heading issues).
- Alt text: media uploads require alt text in the admin (`altTextSchema`, 1–250 characters). Hero slides may override it. Decorative duplicates (lightbox thumbnails, the CTA background) use an empty `alt`.
- Descriptive link text ("Ver habitaciones", "Ver políticas y preguntas frecuentes", room names, "Cómo llegar"). There are no bare "ver más" links. The whole room card links to its type page.
- Internal linking: home → rooms index → each type. Each type links to its siblings, the location page and the policies page. Header and footer link every section, breadcrumbs lead back home, and the 404 page links everywhere.

### Performance (mobile)

Targets: LCP ≤ 2.5 s, CLS ≤ 0.1, TBT ≤ 300 ms (as a proxy for INP), and JavaScript ≤ 170 KB (`lighthouserc.json`).

- **LCP images**: the first hero slide, the first room card of `/habitaciones`, the main room photo and the first gallery photo are `priority` (eager, `fetchpriority="high"`). Each gets a `<link rel=preload>` with the same AVIF `srcset`/`sizes`. The later hero slides stay `hidden`, and the room thumbnails render a placeholder, until `load` (`usePageLoaded`), so they do not compete with the LCP photo.
- **CSS** stays as two cached stylesheets (about 17 KB). `experimental.inlineCss` was measured and rejected: the HTML grew from 26 to 67 KB gzip, because the CSS is also repeated in the RSC payload, and LCP did not improve (the room page got worse).
- **No GSAP on the home**: the hero title reveal is a CSS keyframe (transform only), which saves about 32 KB of JavaScript.
- **Room data**: the room-type list payload now carries `beds` and `areaM2`, so pages no longer fetch every type's detail (`getRoomTypeDetails` is gone). A type page makes two API calls (the list and its own detail).
- **Fonts**: two self-hosted `next/font` files (DM Sans variable, Gilda Display), preloaded with `font-display: swap`, and no Google CDN.
- **Third parties**: the Google Maps iframe loads only after the visitor grants functional cookies or clicks "Cargar mapa".
- **Admin code** stays out of the public bundles. Check the served chunks: no public page loads admin-only chunks.

## 3. How to validate after a deploy

Run these against the production URL, with `SITE_INDEXABLE=true` and `SITE_URL` set.

1. **Flags**: `curl -s https://<domain>/robots.txt` shows `Allow: /` and the sitemap URL. `curl -s https://<domain>/ | grep -o '<meta name="robots"[^>]*>'` must not say `noindex`.
2. **Sitemap**: open `https://<domain>/sitemap.xml`. Every room type is listed and nothing redirects. Check redirects with `curl -sI` on `/contacto`, `/preguntas-frecuentes` and `/servicios` (expect `301`).
3. **Rich Results Test** (<https://search.google.com/test/rich-results>): test the home page, a room type and `/ubicacion`. Expect valid **Breadcrumbs**. Hotel and HotelRoom have no Google rich result, so the tool only lists them as detected items. There must be no "Merchant listings" or "Product snippets" section.
4. **Schema Markup Validator** (<https://validator.schema.org/>): test the same URLs. Expect `Hotel`, `HotelRoom`, `Offer` (when priced), `WebSite` and `BreadcrumbList` with no errors.
5. **Search Console** (<https://search.google.com/search-console>): verify the domain property, submit `https://<domain>/sitemap.xml`, and use URL Inspection on the home page and one room type to request indexing. Over the following weeks, watch the Pages report (indexed vs excluded) and Core Web Vitals.
6. **PageSpeed Insights** (<https://pagespeed.web.dev/>): test the home page and a room type on mobile. Lab data should meet the targets above. Field data (CrUX) appears only once there is enough traffic.
7. **Share preview**: paste the home page and a room URL into WhatsApp, or a link preview debugger. The preview should show the real photo, the title and the description.

Locally: `pnpm --filter @hotel-bambu/e2e lighthouse` (budgets from `lighthouserc.json`). The e2e suite covers titles, canonicals, JSON-LD, NAP, the sitemap and redirects.

## 4. Off-site checklist (owner)

From research §1 and §6. These matter more for "hotel en Mocoa" than anything on the site.

- [ ] **Google Business Profile**: claim and verify it under the exact name "Hotel Bambú Plaza" (no extra keywords). Set the primary category to "Hotel", with as few extra categories as possible. Enter the exact address Calle 8 # 7-10, Mocoa, Putumayo, and the phone +57 310 679 8260. Set the website to the production URL. Add check-in/out times and hotel attributes under Hotel details, and do not set business hours. Upload photos of the façade, reception, every room type, bathrooms and the street, and refresh them regularly.
- [ ] **NAP everywhere, character for character**: use the same name, address and phone as the site footer. Apply this on Booking.com, Expedia/Hotels.com, Airbnb (if used), TripAdvisor, Trip.com, Facebook, Instagram, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Waze, colombia.travel, the Mocoa/Putumayo tourism listings and the RNT record (232175).
- [ ] **Reviews**: after checkout, send a WhatsApp message with the GBP review link. Reply to every review, positive or negative. Never offer incentives without disclosure and never filter who gets asked. Do not copy reviews into the site's JSON-LD.
- [ ] **Data the site still needs** (admin): WhatsApp number, rates per room type (to unlock "desde", `priceRange` and `Offer`), beds and area per type, real type descriptions, nearby places with distances, check-in/out times, the real policies and FAQ (replacing the `[DEMO]` texts), landscape hero photos, real social URLs, and legal approval (`LEGAL_APPROVED=true`).
- [ ] **Google Hotels free booking links**: in GBP, check whether rate editing is offered. If not, a booking engine or channel manager that is a Google connectivity partner is the route.
- [ ] **Links**: partnerships with Fin del Mundo, Indi Wasi and rafting tour operators, and travel bloggers who link to "where to stay".
